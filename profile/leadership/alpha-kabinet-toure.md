# Alpha Kabinet TOURE

**Founder & Chairman, Noether Meridian Inc.**  
**Co-founder, Alpha Stochastic Research (ASR)**

Alpha Kabinet TOURE is the Founder and Chairman of Noether Meridian Inc., a Canadian corporation working across quantitative finance, scientific research, and financial technology. He is also a co-founder of Alpha Stochastic Research (ASR), the company's quantitative research division.

His research interests include stochastic processes, financial mathematics, derivatives, risk and portfolio modelling, and reproducible scientific computing. His institutional responsibilities include corporate governance and the research-to-validation-to-engineering framework.
